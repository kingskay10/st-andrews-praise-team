Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Information collected

The portal may store your name, email address, phone number, location, social handles, role, profile and gallery photos, verses, and finance records entered by authorized staff.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the praise team directory, coordinate worship activities, publish approved updates and reports, and maintain church finance records.

Storage and security

Authentication is provided by Supabase Auth. Access is protected by database row-level security, and uploads are type- and size-checked. Public visitors can see only public content and published reports.

Who can see information

Members can see team directory information and the shared gallery. The chair manages member and event information. The secretary alone can access finance records. Directors can access their department plans and reports. Public visitors can see published updates, events, reports, and the daily verse.

Your choices

To correct or remove your personal information, or to request account deletion, contact the church praise team administrator. Some records may need to be retained for church administration or legal obligations.

Contact

Contact the Praise Team through the email link provided on this site with privacy questions or requests.
